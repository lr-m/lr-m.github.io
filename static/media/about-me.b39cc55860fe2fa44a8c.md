I work in cyber security and have a background in Computer Science. My primary focuses are reverse engineering, vulnerability research & exploit development. However, I also enjoy dabbling in artificial intelligence and how it can be applied to speed up my workflows. Most of my recent projects have been finding and exploiting bugs in cheap embedded devices.
